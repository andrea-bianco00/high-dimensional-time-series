# Research integrity

- Original Taiwan university study: Andrea Bianco, Emidio Grillo and Luca Nudo.
- Beijing Python forecasting is a separate subsequent extension; not part of the original group submission.
- Original Taiwan BigVAR analyses have not been independently rerun here.
- Beijing 2016 was repeatedly inspected during development; results are retrospective and subject to selection bias. 2014, 2015 and 2017 were also consulted in earlier exploration.
- Missing target or prior-hour PM2.5 observations are excluded from scoring.
- Cross-station data latency at inference time is unverified.
- Negative forecasts remain included in the published metrics.
- Clean-environment reproducibility and independent prospective evaluation are not yet established.
- Do not equate 12-station Beijing forecasting with 516-dimensional Taiwan modeling.
